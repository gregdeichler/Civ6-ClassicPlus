# Known limitations

**0.2 is an in-game preview.** Database postconditions passed against a Gathering Storm snapshot and the new grievance/flood rules passed against an in-memory copy of the current Windows/Proton game cache. Grievance generation, AI reactions, flooding, era transitions, the Medieval Congress boundary, DLC combinations, and save/reload need engine verification for this build. Stock game modes and multiplayer are outside the initial test baseline. Dramatic Ages is suppressed in setup because it deliberately reintroduces ages; leave it off in any imported configuration.

The 2026-11-20 local patch fixes a reported initial-load database failure caused by an optional Persia requirement, and replaces the unsupported ruleset criterion with the installed game's game-core criterion. Regression checks pass with and without that optional requirement; a successful engine load of the patched build is still pending. See [testing](testing.md) for the evidence and its limits.

The game may still have hard-coded or ungated entry points: the historian keyboard shortcut, stale age or Favor descriptions, Favor deal widgets, and the diplomacy ranking tab are smoke-test targets. Congress capabilities and its regular start era are changed in gameplay, rather than just hiding its button. The Favor database type, engine trade code, and dormant catalog entries remain. Some alternate reward text (e.g. Monarchy, future civics, Carbon Recapture, and former diplomatic-victory wonders) still needs a pass. Congress-dependent competitions and emergencies are intentionally excluded from this preview. The effects of the free promise requests on AI valuation are unverified. Dark Age cards remain defined and should be inaccessible; check the policy and Civilopedia screens after an era transition.

Grievance source amounts, war/capture/raze multipliers, AI grievance valuation, and the two stock grievance relationship penalties are neutralized. A stock -100% grievance-generation modifier is attached to the default major-leader and city-state traits as an additional guard. This does not restore base-game warmonger rules, remove old grievance history, or hide every stock grievance widget. Ordinary anger from agendas, denunciations, broken promises, occupation and razing can still exist independently of grievance totals. Grievance-only abilities become redundant; no replacement balance bonuses were added.

River and sea-level events have zero frequency at all standard disaster settings. River-flood warming bonuses, damage, and fertility rewards are also zero. The stock map utility receives a zero coastal-lowland percentage for newly generated maps. Floodplain terrain, dams, hydropower, and other disasters remain. Flood-triggered benefits (including Great Bath Faith growth) no longer trigger. Disabling sea-level events also prevents their bundled ice-loss and late-climate fertility effects from those events. Existing saves, premarked custom-map lowlands, Apocalypse/forced events, and third-party disaster scripts are not verified; this does not repair already submerged tiles.

Classic+ still uses governors, strategic stockpiles, and electricity. In particular:

- A governor capability exists. The data audit found Seondeok, original Suleiman and Mapuche dependencies; title rewards, loyalty pressure, governors granted by modes, and AI allocation need a separate pass.
- `Units.StrategicResource` and `Units_XP2.ResourceCost` / maintenance columns coexist. Their presence does not prove the original resource-access rule runs under Gathering Storm. A change to production, purchase, or upgrade costs needs in-game tests with both zero access and an improved source, including AI behavior. No resource costs were changed.
- `Buildings_XP2` exposes power demand, fuel and powered amenities; `Building_YieldChangesBonusWithPower` holds conditional yields. Power also touches regional yields, fuel improvements, disasters and emissions, so converting those two tables alone is insufficient.

English descriptions are updated selectively. Other locales retain stock descriptions. UI mods replacing ActionPanel or PartialScreenHooks, or rules mods writing the same capabilities, traits, or victory setup, can conflict. Existing saves and other rulesets are unsupported.
